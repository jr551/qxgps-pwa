QXGPS Lite is an unofficial, ad-free viewer for GPS trackers that use the QXGPS / QXHL vendor cloud. It wraps the same open-source web app in a minimal Android shell — no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no Google Play Services.

Features:
• Live map and device status
• History tracks with playback
• Device details (read-only)
• Alarm list and optional notifications

This app is strictly read-only toward the vendor platform: it does not send device commands or change configuration. You need your own tracker account.
